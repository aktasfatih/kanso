Why Kanso

Built to feel instant, on a server you already run

Kanso isn’t a fork. It’s a kanban board written from scratch around a few performance decisions, and it lives inside your Nextcloud.

01

Fast by design

A card move is a single-row update, never a bulk renumber. Summary-only payloads, ETag caching and virtualized columns stay smooth past 2,000 cards.

02

Live for everyone

Changes show up for everyone on the board through notify_push when your server has it, with a light polling fallback everywhere else.

03

Yours, not rented

Kanso runs on your own Nextcloud and keeps its data in its own tables. Your users and groups already work. No lock-in, no per-seat pricing.

Kanso (簡素) is one of the seven principles of Japanese Zen aesthetics. It means simplicity: the deliberate elimination of clutter, so that only what matters remains.

Features

Everything a team board needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

All of this ships today. The full, always-current list lives in the project README.

Cards

Cards that hold the whole task

Open a card and everything about the work is in one place.

  • Markdown descriptions, labels, due dates, assignees and priorities
  • Checklists and parent ↔ child cards — a parent completes when its children are done
  • Threaded comments with @mentions, and watchers on a card, a thread or a whole board
  • Attachments up to 100 MiB, custom fields, time tracking and private reminders

Views

Board, list or timeline

Switch per board — Kanso remembers your choice.

  • A Timeline (Gantt) that plots cards by start → due, with due-only cards as milestones
  • A dense List view for scanning hundreds of cards
  • Swimlanes by assignee, label or priority, and saved filters to come back to

Analytics

See how work actually flows

Per board and per project, with no setup.

  • Velocity with trend, cycle time and throughput
  • Breakdowns by column, priority, assignee and label
  • Overdue, aging and checklist-progress signals

And more

The rest of the toolbox

Workflow, integrations and the small things that make a board pleasant to live in.

  • Review workflow

    Request a review, then approve or request changes. Custom review types, and an optional done-gate that holds a card until every review is approved.

  • Automation

    Stack roles and WIP limits, recurring cards on RRULE schedules, auto-archive rules and card templates.

  • My Work & Projects

    One hub for your tasks, the reviews waiting on you and an inbox of mentions — plus Projects that collect cards across boards.

  • Sharing

    Per-user and per-group access control, and an optional read-only public link that needs no Nextcloud account.

  • In your calendar

    Every board you can read appears as a read-only CalDAV calendar of its due-dated cards, in Nextcloud Calendar, Tasks and on your phone.

  • Email intake

    Point a board at an IMAP mailbox and incoming mail lands as cards, with an optional sender allowlist.

  • Code links

    Attach pull requests and issues to a card. GitHub and Forgejo webhooks move cards as work opens, merges and closes.

  • Import, export, back up

    Import from Deck, Trello JSON or CSV. Export any board as a zip with its attachments, and let admins schedule backups.

  • Keyboard-first

    A command palette with full-text search across cards and comments, undo toasts, and a trash with restore.

Install

Up and running in a minute

Kanso is on the Nextcloud App Store, so an administrator can install it without touching the shell.

  1. In Nextcloud, open Apps and find Kanso under Organization.
  2. Click Download and enable.
  3. Open Kanso from the app menu and create your first board.
or from the command line
$ cd /path/to/nextcloud
$ sudo -u www-data php occ app:install kanso
